PLOT | BEFORE YOU BUILD
Your first project conversation checklist

1. Your land
- Plot dimensions and site location
- Ownership and boundary documents for review by the appropriate professionals
- Site access, slope and known ground or drainage concerns
- Existing services, neighbouring buildings and photographs

2. Your vision
- Intended use, number of rooms and how the spaces should work
- Essential requirements versus optional features
- Reference images and material preferences
- Accessibility and future expansion needs

3. Your budget
- Indicative total budget and funding plan
- Separate allowances for design, approvals, construction, utilities and furnishings
- Contingency to be discussed against project-specific risks

4. Your programme
- Desired start and completion windows
- Decisions that must be made before site work
- Availability for design reviews and approvals

5. Your team and scope
- Architectural and structural drawing requirements
- Survey, ground investigation and approval requirements to confirm locally
- BOQ, procurement, supervision and reporting responsibilities
- Written deliverables, exclusions, fees and change procedures

This checklist prepares a conversation. It is not a design, quotation or approval.
